# Settings and backups

## Fast flag cleanup

Since September 2025 Roblox only reads a short list of fast flags (their [allowlist](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/allowlist-for-local-client-configuration-via-fast-flags/3966569)) and ignores the rest. Old flag lists from TikTok or Discord are mostly flags that do nothing now.

Settings > Engine Settings tells you how many of your flags Roblox ignores. "Remove the flags Roblox ignores" shows you the list and takes them out if you say yes. Hit Save after.

## Fast flag profiles

Also on Engine Settings. Save your whole flag list under a name, then load it again later. Handy for switching between, say, an AFK set and a playing set, or for keeping a copy before you clean up. Loading a profile replaces the flags you have now, and it's used once you press Save.

## Mod profiles

On the Mods page. Set your mods up how you want (font, sounds, sky, cursor...), type a name and press "Save current mods". Then give a game that mod profile on the [Gameplay](Gameplay.md#game-profiles) page. Fast flags aren't part of mod profiles.

## Coming from another strap

On the PSYstrap page. If Bloxstrap, Fishstrap, Froststrap, Voidstrap or Nyxstrap is installed on your PC, pick it and press Import. It copies:

- settings that PSYstrap also has (same name), except the look (theme, bootstrapper style)
- your fast flags
- your mod files (fonts, sounds, textures and so on)

Press Save afterwards to keep the settings and flags. The mod files are copied straight away.

## Backups

Also on the PSYstrap page. "Save a backup" puts everything in one zip: settings, macros, smart macros, mods, fast flags, flag and mod profiles, playtime and recent servers. "Restore a backup" puts it all back, for example on a new PC. Close and reopen Settings after restoring.

Saved accounts are never in a backup. They're encrypted so they only work on the PC (and Windows user) that saved them, so you just log in again on the new PC.
